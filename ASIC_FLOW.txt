3. D flipflop:
	o DFF captures the D input at the positive edge of the clock and assigns to Q.
	
	o Setup time:
		o minimum time before the active edge of the clock for which duration, D input must be stable. If this condition does not meet, Q may or may not captures the value of D. This is called as mertastability. Where we don't know whether Q takes 0 or 1.
			o Analogy: flight boarding time is steup time.
				o If we arrive 45 min before boarding time, we  will surly say we will catch the flight (1). else we might miss(0)
				o If we arrive 45 min after. we may or may not catch flight.
			o this condition where we can't predit the ouput is called meta stable.

				o For simulation purpose, we represents it as x
					o this 'x' can can be 0 or 1.
				o if D input does'nt meet the setup time requirement , the ouput enters into a meta stable state.
				o where it can be take value '1' or '0'.
		o what is the issue with meta stability?
			o flight analogy.
			o hyd to New york through 4 connections.
				o hyd --> delhi--> hongkong--> Dubai --> new york

				o if we enter meta stability at hyd. (we may or may not catch the flight.)
			o VLSI circuits: one flipflop going tho meta stability (unknown state), this meta stability spreads to whole circuit with in few clock cycles.
				o whole design enters into unknown state.
			o If we reach hyd with proper boarding time then we won't have metastability.
	
	o We can visualize VLSI circuits as below.

---> Parasitic Capacitance:
	o unwanted capacitance at the output of every gate.
	o what factors impact parasitic capacitance?
			o Fanout at the output of the gate.
		o Capacitance is the ability to store the charge.
	o Why it is impartant for PD engineer?
		o As parasitic capacitance croses a threshold value, RC circuit delay increse.
		o which impcats the timing behaviour.
		o PD engineer may have to use techniques to reduce the parasitic value
			o Using buffers.
o Fanout:
	o Number of gates driven by a cretain net, it's the load they are putting on the net.

o Clock distribution (part of CTS)
	o Concept of routing clocks to all the flip flops in the design.

o Skew:
	o Relative difference in time, when the clock is reaching one flipflop and next fliflop.

	 o types of skew:
	 	o Gblobal skew:
			o check the skew for globally, (non related flops.)
		o Local skew:
			o checks the skew between 2 related flipflops.



==> Reset Distribution:
	o Reset is a very impartant signal(port) in any IC.
	o Design can enter in to unknown or unwanted state for many reason.
		o How to bring it back to a known state?
			o By using reset we can bring it back to known state.
	o How to apply reset to all flops?
		o what factors to be considerd?
			o All FFs should recive reset with minimal skew.
		o every chip has clock and reset manager block. which gets reset from the external world.this reset maanager further distributes the reset to complete chip.

			o Table for cell dealy
			o Table output transistion.

			o in cadence it is tech_lef
